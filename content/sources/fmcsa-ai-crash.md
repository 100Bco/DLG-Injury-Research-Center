The deepest national source on crashes involving large trucks and buses. A&I reports crash rates, fatalities, and towaway crashes, with a state dropdown that produces Texas-specific reports and a visualization tool for building custom views.

Because it isolates commercial-vehicle crashes, it is the right source for anything involving 18-wheelers, commercial trucks, or buses — a distinct dataset from general crash statistics.

**A note on currency:** the underlying MCMIS data is considered preliminary for 22 months as reports continue to arrive, so recent-year figures will shift.

**Best for:** commercial truck and bus crash analysis at the state or national level.
