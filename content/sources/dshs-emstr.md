A library of infographics and surveillance reports built from the Texas trauma registry — the health system's record of injuries serious enough to reach hospitals, which fatality-only sources miss.

Published items include "Texas Hospital Motor Vehicle Injuries" (April 2024), "Motor-Vehicle Traffic Trauma Involving Children Age 5 and Under, 2021–2023," and "2023 Commercial Motor Vehicle Traffic Incidents." The registry draws on more than five million records a year from EMS, trauma facilities, and medical examiners.

**Best for:** hospital-level injury data and ready-made infographics — the severity picture between "minor" and "fatal."
