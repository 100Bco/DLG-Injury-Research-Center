STSI is an interactive portal that visualizes FARS data as charts and GIS maps, offering a faster read on core measures like drunk driving, seat-belt use, and speeding than querying raw FARS.

It provides both state-level and county-level reports for ten consecutive years, filtered to Texas.

**Best for:** a quick visual pull of Texas fatality indicators, especially when county-level breakdowns are needed.
