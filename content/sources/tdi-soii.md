The companion to CFOI, SOII counts nonfatal workplace injuries and illnesses in Texas, broken down by industry (NAICS). It provides the base for estimating the risk of work-vehicle crashes that lead to disabling injury rather than death.

**Best for:** nonfatal occupational injury rates by industry — useful for workers' comp and commercial-vehicle risk context.
