The standard national reference for how civil disputes actually resolve. It documents that trials account for only about 3% of tort, contract, and property dispositions in state general-jurisdiction courts, and that tort claims make up 61% of civil trials.

**A note on currency:** the 2005 Civil Justice Survey of State Courts is the most recent comprehensive national dataset of its kind, so cite it with its year.

**Best for:** the widely-cited baseline on trial rates vs. settlement — useful context for how rarely injury cases reach a verdict.
