The top-level hub for all of TxDOT's crash reporting resources. It links out to cumulative crash statistics covering 2003–2024, the crash report forms themselves, and instructions for requesting data.

**Best for:** orienting yourself before diving in — a starting point that shows the full range of what TxDOT publishes and how to access each piece.
