CFOI is the authoritative count of work-related deaths in Texas, produced with the US Bureau of Labor Statistics. It matters for injury research because it breaks out transportation and material-moving incidents — the leading cause of workplace fatalities — and reports separately on driver/sales workers and truck drivers.

**A key figure:** Texas recorded 557 fatal occupational injuries in 2024, with transportation incidents prominent among them.

**Best for:** the overlap of workplace injury, commercial driving, and workers' compensation — deaths that occur on the job, including on the road.
